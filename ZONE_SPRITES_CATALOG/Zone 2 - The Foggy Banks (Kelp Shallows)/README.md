# 🌫️ Zone 2: The Foggy Banks (Kelp Shallows)

- **Radial Distance**: `1200 to 2400 px` from Central Safe Harbor
- **Encounter Depth**: `30 to 60 meters`
- **Atmosphere & Water**: Cold bruised teal/grey water (`#0a1f2e`), drifting volumetric fog banks, obscured moon, moderate wave amplitude.
- **Main Catch Encounter**: **Blighted Pike** (`fish_shadowy_trout_s2.png`)
