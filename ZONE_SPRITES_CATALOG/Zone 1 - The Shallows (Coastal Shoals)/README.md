# 🌊 Zone 1: The Shallows (Coastal Shoals)

- **Radial Distance**: `0 to 1200 px` from Central Safe Harbor
- **Encounter Depth**: `0 to 30 meters`
- **Atmosphere & Water**: Calm murky brackish green water (`#0e2b24`), gentle rolling mist, high visibility, warm lighthouse beam.
- **Main Catch Encounter**: **Innsmouth Perch** (`fish_shadowy_perch_s6.png`)
