# ⚡ Zone 3: The Sunken Reefs (Abyssal Trench)

- **Radial Distance**: `2400 to 3600 px` from Central Safe Harbor
- **Encounter Depth**: `60 to 100 meters`
- **Atmosphere & Water**: Pitch-slate abyssal ocean (`#060c18`), storm breaker waves, lightning storm squalls, heavy rain streaks, deep trench rumbles.
